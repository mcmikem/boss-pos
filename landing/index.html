BEFORE YOU TAKE CHARGE

Ask everything first.

Can I sell when the internet is down?

Yes. Sales are saved on the device and sync when the connection returns. The app shows you what is still pending.

Do I need new hardware?

No. Start with the Android phone, tablet, or computer you already use. Add a scanner or printer later if it earns its place.

Can I use mobile money and cash?

Yes. Cash, MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, and credit are first-class payment methods in the till.

How long does setup take?

One sitting: create the shop, set a PIN, add your first products, make a sale.

What if my phone breaks or gets stolen?

Your data lives on our secure server, not just your phone. Every sale syncs whenever you're online, and the server keeps daily snapshots of the whole shop. Get a new phone, open your till link, enter your PIN — your stock, sales, and history are all there.

How do I back up without internet?

You don't have to think about it. Every sale is saved on the device the second it happens, then syncs automatically when the connection returns. On top of that the server snapshots your shop daily, and you can download a full backup anytime from Settings.

What stops my attendant pocketing cash or voiding sales?

Three locks: every sale is stamped with the seller's name, refunds and deleted sales need your manager PIN, and the end-of-day count compares drawer cash against sales — so shortages show up the same evening, not month-end.

How do I know when to restock from Kikuubo?

Set a low-stock level per product. BOSS flags it on the till, pings your phone, and lists everything to reorder on the daily close-out — before the shelf empties.

Is BOSS EFRIS-ready?

Yes — with no extra typing at the till. The honest version: going live takes three things. (1) URA approves your shop's device under your TIN — 1–2 days, and we walk you through it on WhatsApp. (2) Your goods must be registered in EFRIS with matching codes. (3) Live filing goes through a fiscal endpoint (URA or an aggregator) — if they charge, that's separate from your BOSS plan. Rehearse the whole flow free in sandbox mode first, and know this: filing never blocks a sale — failures queue for retry.

I'm already selling with BOSS — does anything change?

No. Your till link, PIN, stock, and sales history stay exactly the same. This page is for new shops — keep opening your till from the same bookmark on your phone.
