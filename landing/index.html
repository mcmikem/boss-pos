Why I built this

Kampala

I ran shops as a young man. Small businesses, one after another, starting with almost nothing. Some made money. Every one lost money too. Not to bad luck — to the same three things that eat every small shop in Uganda: credit customers who disappear, stock that walks off the shelf, and a drawer that never quite matches what you remember selling.

I couldn't afford the tools that big businesses use. So I built my own. BOSS POS is what I wish I had then: a till that knows every customer by name, remembers what they owe, sends them an invoice automatically, and closes the day with you knowing exactly where the money went.

Not a company guessing what small shops need. Just the thing I needed.

The problem

Every shop loses money it can't account for.

Not big losses. Small ones, every day, that add up to a month of profit gone.

The credit book

You gave 50k on credit. They said next week. It's been a month. Their number changed. You lost the money and the goods.

You took MoMo. But is it in your phone, the shop phone, or the attendant's phone? Three places to check. One number that never adds up.

The closing count

What BOSS does

Three things paper can't.

Every sale, every payment

Cash, MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, credit. One screen. Every sale carries the seller's name. Refunds need your PIN.

A credit book that follows up

Every customer who takes credit gets a name, a limit, and a history. When you record a credit sale, BOSS sends them an invoice on WhatsApp. When the limit is reached, the till stops them.

Stock down to the last piece

Set a low-stock level for each product. BOSS alerts you on the till and lists everything to reorder before the shelf empties.

Questions

Things shop owners ask.

Does it work when there's no internet?

Yes. Sales save on the phone and sync when the network comes back. You can see what's still waiting to sync, so you're never guessing.

What if my attendant is the problem?

Every sale carries the seller's name. Refunds and deleted sales need your PIN, not theirs. The close-out shows you if the drawer is short — that evening, not month-end.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. Message me on WhatsApp. I'll set up a trial till for your shop, load a few products, and you can sell on it for 3 days before you decide.

What's the difference between monthly and annual?

Monthly is 20,000 a month. Annual is 185,000 a year — that works out to 15,400 a month, saving you 55,000 over the year. Same features in both. Cancel anytime.

What does the first-25-shops rate mean?

The first 25 shops pay 12,000 a month — locked at that price forever, even when we raise prices for everyone else. It's our thank-you for believing in BOSS early.

How do customer profiles and reminders work?

Every regular customer gets a profile — name, phone, purchase history, credit balance. They can subscribe to updates from your shop to hear about new stock and discounts. BOSS sends them invoices and reminders automatically, and sends you a summary every evening.

Do I need a scanner or printer?

No. Your phone is the till. Receipts and invoices go out on WhatsApp. If you want a scanner or printer later, I can add one.

My phone gets stolen. Then what?

Your shop data lives on our server, not just on the phone. Get a new phone, open your link, enter your PIN. Stock, sales, credit, customer profiles — all still there.

Can I run more than one till?

Yes. Multi-till sync means two tills, two shops — one stock count, one credit book, one customer list. Always in sync. Same price. No extra charge.

Does BOSS do EFRIS receipts?

BOSS is built for small shops, not tax compliance. If you need EFRIS, message me on WhatsApp and I'll tell you honestly whether BOSS is right for you.

What if I stop paying?

You keep read-only access to your sales, stock, credit and customer history. You can export everything. You just can't ring new sales until you renew.

How do I pay?

MTN MoMo or Airtel Money. No card needed. Monthly or annual, your choice.

I already use BOSS. Does anything change?

No. Your till link, PIN, stock, sales history and customer profiles stay the same. This page is for new shops — keep opening your till from the same bookmark.
