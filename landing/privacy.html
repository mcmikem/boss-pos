YOUR SHOP, YOUR DATA

Privacy policy

LAST UPDATED · SEPTEMBER 2026

What we store to run your till

Only what the till needs to work:

  • Shop records you enter — products, sales, expenses, suppliers, staff names and roles, and till settings.
  • Contact details — your shop name plus the WhatsApp number or email you use to talk to us.
  • PINs, stored hashed — till and staff PINs are kept as one-way hashes with rate-limiting, never as readable text.

What stays on your device

Every sale is saved on your phone, tablet, or computer the second it happens, and syncs to our server when the connection returns. The server also keeps daily snapshots so a lost or stolen phone never means lost records — open your till link on a new device, enter your PIN, and your stock, sales, and history are there. You can download a full backup anytime from Settings.

What we never do

  • No selling your data. Your shop records are never sold or shared for marketing.
  • Google Sheets only if you connect it. Sales and expenses are sent to your sheet only when you paste in your own sheet URL.
  • URA filing only if you switch it on. Fiscal invoices go to EFRIS under your shop's own TIN only on plans with EFRIS enabled.

Your control

  • Export your full shop data anytime from Settings.
  • Ask for a correction or full deletion and we will do it — message us on WhatsApp or email.
