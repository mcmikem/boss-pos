Questions before switching

Good software should explain itself.

Can I sell when the internet is down?

Yes. Sales are saved on the device and sync when the connection returns. The app shows you when changes are still pending.

Do I need new hardware?

No. Start with the Android phone, tablet, or computer you already use. Add a scanner or printer later if it earns its place.

Can I use mobile money and cash?

Yes. Cash, MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, and credit are first-class payment methods in the till.

How long does setup take?

You can create the shop, set a PIN, add your first products, and make a sale in one sitting.
